Optimized through product stacking strategies, storage protocols, and seasonal routine switching. Secondary goal: Maximize The Ordinary sales incentives through order splitting.

### Storage & Stability Protocols

| Product | Storage | Reason | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| **C-Glow (Vitamin C)** | Refrigerate | Oxidizes rapidly | Permanent fridge |
| **Retinoid 2% Emulsion** | Fridge (open) or Cool Drawer (sealed) | Heat sensitive | Open: fridge; Backup: cool drawer |
| **Resveratrol + Ferulic** | Cool/Dark Drawer | Never refrigerate (crystallizes) | Room temp only |
| **Rose Hip Seed Oil** | Cool/Dark Drawer | Prevent solidification | Room temp only |
| **All other serums** | Cool/Dark Drawer | Standard stability | Room temp acceptable |

### Routine Architecture

**HOME ROUTINE (Alternating Schedule) — Aggressive Anti-Aging**

| Frequency | AM (Morning) | PM (Evening) |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon/Wed/Fri** | Cleanser → Lash Serum → Caffeine → Alpha Arbutin + Niacinamide + Sunscreen | Cleanser → Argireline → Buffet + Copper Peptides + Rose Hip Oil |
| **Tue/Thu/Sat** | Cleanser → Lash Serum → Caffeine → C-Glow → Resveratrol + Niacinamide + Sunscreen | Cleanser → Granactive Retinoid 2% + Rose Hip Oil |
| **Sunday** | (Choose either stack) | Cleanser → Exfoliating Acid → Niacinamide + Rose Hip Oil |

**CHINA ROUTINE (2 Months) — Pollution Defense**

Daily consistent protocol (no alternating):
- **AM:** Cleanser → Lash Serum + Caffeine → Ascorbyl Glucoside (Stable Vit C) → Resveratrol + Arbutin → Niacinamide + Sunscreen (OilControl)
- **PM:** Cleanser → Argireline → Regular Buffet (not Copper) → Niacinamide + Moisturizer
